◈ Questions

Before you book.

What does a project actually cost?

The figures above are starting points, not quotes. Real cost depends on scope, integrations, and how much of the surrounding system we touch. After a strategy call you get a fixed written proposal with a breakdown by phase — no hourly surprises.

How quickly can you start?

Discovery usually begins within two weeks of a signed scope. For urgent work we can often start a focused discovery sprint sooner.

Do you work with our existing team?

Often, yes. We work alongside in-house engineers and designers, and a handover that leaves your team able to run what we built is part of every engagement, not an afterthought.

Who owns the code and the designs?

You do, on final payment. Source, design files, infrastructure definitions and documentation transfer to you in full.

What happens after launch?

Every tier includes a support window. Beyond that we offer an ongoing retainer for monitoring, iteration and roadmap work — or we hand over cleanly and step back.

Can you sign an NDA?

Yes. Send yours over before the first call, or use ours.
