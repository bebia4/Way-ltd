How we handle your data

This notice explains what Way International Services Limited collects when you use this site or get in touch, why we collect it, and what you can ask us to do with it.

Last updated 2026.

What we collect

Only what you send us. If you submit the contact form or email us, we receive your name, company, email address, and whatever you write in your brief. We do not ask for anything else.

Why we use it

To answer your enquiry, prepare a proposal, and carry out any engagement that follows. We do not sell your details, and we do not add you to a marketing list without you asking for it.

Analytics and cookies

This site sets no advertising or tracking cookies and embeds no third-party trackers. Webfonts are served from this domain rather than a third party, so visiting the site does not hand your IP address to an external font provider.

If analytics are added later, this notice will be updated to name the provider and what it records before it goes live.

How long we keep it

Enquiry correspondence is kept while it is commercially relevant and then deleted. Project records are kept for as long as contract and tax obligations require.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Write to the address below and we will respond within one month.

Processors

We use third-party providers for email and hosting. They process data on our instructions only. We can name the current providers on request.

Contact

Questions about this page? Write to hello@wayinternational.services.
